# Music

no attribution required. Credits are given anyway. Files were re-encoded to OGG Vorbis (q3; the later additions at q2, cut to two minutes).

SHA256SUMS.txt). Per its notices, Abstraction / Tallbeard Studios (Ben Burnes) asks, without adding a
restriction to CC0, that the music not be used for NFTs, AI/ML, or resold as unmodified assets.
Check the source pages before shipping if you need certainty.
